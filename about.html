About COSMORA

The universe belongs
to everyone.

COSMORA turns decades of space science into an experience anyone can explore — no telescope, textbook or physics degree required.

01

Our mission

Every image from Hubble or Webb, every rover selfie from Mars, belongs to all of humanity. Yet most of it sits in archives and research papers. COSMORA exists to bring those discoveries into the open and make space exploration visually engaging and accessible to everyone, wherever they are.

“Somewhere, something incredible is waiting to be known.”— Often attributed to Carl Sagan

02

Designed to inspire

We think wonder is the best way into science. That is why COSMORA uses cinematic visuals, living orbits and interactive galleries, so that learning feels like exploring. Each planet, galaxy and mission is shown with clear, carefully sourced facts.

Explore

An animated solar system, a deep-field gallery and a mission log you can wander freely.

Understand

Plain-language explanations with the real numbers behind every world and wonder.

Belong

Built for students, dreamers and lifelong stargazers, on any screen, anywhere.

03

Real science, real images

The gallery and mission photographs come from the NASA Image and Video Library, which credits the teams behind Hubble, Webb, Spitzer, Chandra and many more. Every image links back to its original source.
